ABOUT BMW MOTORSPORT

BMW M, also known as BMW Motorsport, was created in 1972 to facilitate BMW's world-class racing program. Over the decades, it transformed into a symbol of unmatched road performance and precision engineering.

The letter "M" represents the most powerful letter in the automotive world. Every BMW M model comes equipped with track-tuned suspension, aerodynamic body styling, high-revving TwinPower Turbo engines, and a driver-centric cockpit.
